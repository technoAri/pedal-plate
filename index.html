Our Story

Good food shouldn't be expensive, complicated, or far away.

Pedal Plate started with a simple idea: bring the comfort of home-cooked food closer to people — one bicycle at a time.

We celebrate the everyday meals we grew up with, made with care and served fresh, locally and affordably. Because sometimes, the best food is simply home food, on the move.

  1. We cook

    Home-style recipes, made fresh every day in small batches.

  2. We pedal

    Our cycle carts carry it, hot and sealed, to a street near you.

  3. You eat

    Walk up, pay, and enjoy a proper meal — no app, no wait.
