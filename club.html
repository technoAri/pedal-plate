How Pedal Club works

  1. Join here, or ask for your bill on WhatsApp at any Pedal Plate cart. Your card link comes with your bill.
  2. Every combo you buy at the cart adds a stamp, up to 2 a day.
  3. After 9 stamps, your 10th combo is free. Show this card at the cart to claim it: the rider scans the QR or you read out the 4-digit code.

Free to join. A card is valid for 30 days from its first stamp; after that, or after your free meal, the next plate starts a new card on the same number. Not for corporate, bulk or home delivery orders. Keep your card link to yourself: anyone with it can see your code. To stop messages or delete your number, WhatsApp us on +91 76991 49899.
