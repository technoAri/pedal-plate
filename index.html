Our Story

Good food shouldn't have to be expensive, complicated, or far away

Pedal Plate started with a simple idea: bring the comfort of home-cooked food closer to people — one bicycle at a time. We celebrate the everyday meals we grew up with, made with care and served fresh, locally and affordably.

We cook. We pedal. You eat.

Because sometimes, the best food is simply home food, on the move.
