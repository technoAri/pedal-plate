Privacy

This page explains what we measure on pedalplate.in and why.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics and Vercel Web Analytics to understand how people find and use our website, so we can improve it. They tell us things like which pages are visited, roughly which city or country visitors are in (based on IP address), the type of device, and which buttons are used (for example "Call us" or "Request a quote").

Google Analytics uses cookies. Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights do not use cookies. We don't use this data to identify you personally, and we don't sell it.

Enquiry form

When you send an enquiry, your details open in your own email app and are sent to us only if you choose to send that email. We use them only to reply to you.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.
